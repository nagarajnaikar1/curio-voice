Curio Voice

Free · unlimited · runs on your phone

Tap Generate. The first time, the free voice model (~90 MB) downloads once; use Wi-Fi.

Saves a WAV file. Upload it to Claude to sync it with your video.

Tips

• Keep the screen on and the app open while it works. Long scripts take a few minutes on a phone.
• After the first download it works offline.
• Deep narrator voices: am_onyx, am_fenrir, am_michael, bm_george.
• Voices from Kokoro (Apache-2.0): free for commercial use.
