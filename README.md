# 📱 Curio Voice (Android app)

Free, unlimited narration that runs **on your phone**. Kokoro-82M (Apache-2.0) runs inside the app, with no account and no credits.

**Install:** open **Releases** (right side of this page) → tap **CurioVoice.apk** → open the download → allow "Install unknown apps" for your browser or Files app when Android asks → **Install**.

**First use:** tap **▶ Preview voice** on Wi-Fi. The voice model (~90 MB) downloads once and is cached.

**Saving:** *Save audio* puts the WAV in **Downloads/CurioVoice**. *Share* sends it to WhatsApp, Drive, Claude and so on.

Every push to `main` rebuilds the APK automatically (GitHub Actions). Android 10 or newer is required.
